* Logistics-Delivery-Delay-Analysis-Dashboard.

* Project Overview

The Logistics Delivery Delay Analysis Dashboard analyzes order delivery performance across multiple warehouses to understand the frequency and patterns of delivery delays.The goal of this project is to evaluate delivery efficiency, identify warehouses experiencing higher delays, and understand which product categories are most affected by delayed deliveries.

This analysis helps logistics managers and operations teams monitor delivery performance and identify areas that require improvement in order fulfillment and distribution processes.

* Business Problem

In the logistics industry, delivery delays can lead to customer dissatisfaction, increased operational costs, and reduced service quality. Companies need to monitor their delivery performance regularly to ensure orders are delivered on time and to quickly identify operational inefficiencies.

This project analyzes delivery delay data to identify patterns in delays across warehouses and product categories, helping businesses improve logistics operations and customer satisfaction.

*  Dataset Description

The dataset contains information about customer orders and delivery performance across different warehouses. The data includes details about order counts, delivery delay days, delay categories, product categories, and warehouse locations.

The analysis focuses on understanding delivery timeliness and identifying the factors contributing to delayed shipments.


* Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Orders: 2,000

* Average Delay Days: 0.77 days

* On-Time Delivery Rate: 55.7%

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall delivery performance of the logistics operations.


* Key Business Questions

1. What percentage of orders are delivered on time compared to delayed deliveries?
2. Which warehouse has the highest average delivery delay?
3. Which product categories experience the most delivery delays?
4. How does delivery delay distribution vary across warehouses?
5. What proportion of orders fall into short delays (1–2 days) versus long delays (3+ days)?

* Key Insights

* More than 55% of orders are delivered on time, indicating moderate delivery efficiency.
* However, approximately 44% of deliveries experience delays, suggesting that operational improvements are needed.
* Abuja warehouse has the highest average delay (0.88 days) and also records the highest number of delayed orders.
* Most delays fall within the 1–2 day delay category, which suggests that delays are often short but frequent.
* Fashion products appear most frequently in delayed deliveries, which may be related to higher demand or supply chain constraints.


* Recommendations

1. Improve operational efficiency in Abuja warehouse since it records the highest delay levels.
2. Investigate the causes of 1–2 day delays, as they represent the largest share of delayed orders.
3. Monitor logistics processes for high-demand product categories such as fashion to minimize delivery delays.
4. Improve coordination between warehouses and transportation teams to reduce short delivery disruptions.
5. Implement performance monitoring systems to track delivery delays and quickly resolve operational bottlenecks.
